01 / About
01—∞

Learning in public. Building through experimentation. Improving through feedback.

I'm pursuing a B.E. in Computer Science and Engineering at Vidyavardhaka College of Engineering (VVCE), Mysuru. My current academic record is 8.70 CGPA through Semester II.

I enjoy taking on open-ended technical problems: understanding the constraints, mapping the system, building a working slice, and documenting what is—and is not—ready. My projects span developer tools, privacy, career preparation, campus platforms, meeting workflows, and local-first applications.

I'm also interested in the business side of technology: identifying real user pain points, testing assumptions early, and building products that can scale responsibly. I value honest engineering over inflated claims; prototypes should be presented as prototypes, and production readiness should be earned through testing.
